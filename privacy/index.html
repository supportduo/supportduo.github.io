SupportDuo Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 October 2026

The short version

  • SupportDuo helps NDIS participants and independent support workers find each other and work together. To do that we need some personal information, including information about disability and support needs.
  • We only collect what the app needs, we never sell it, we show no ads, and we use no advertising, analytics or AI services.
  • Other people only see what they need: your exact location, gender, interests list, NDIS number and safety details are never shown to people you are not connected with.
  • Your information is stored with our hosting provider in Singapore. Some service providers (for notifications and app store billing) are in the United States.
  • You can see and change most of your information in the app, and delete your account at any time in the app or by emailing contactsupportduo@gmail.com.
  • Questions or complaints: email contactsupportduo@gmail.com. You can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

1. About this policy

The SupportDuo app is run by SupportDuo (ABN 18 527 561 017), a registered Australian business ("we", "us"). This policy explains how we collect, hold, use and share personal information when you use the SupportDuo app or contact us. It is our privacy policy under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs), which we follow. Because SupportDuo is used to arrange care and support for people with disability, we treat the information in it as health information and also follow state and territory health records laws where they apply.

Support workers who use SupportDuo are independent businesses, not our employees. Each worker is also responsible, under privacy law and the NDIS Code of Conduct, for the participant information they hold, such as their own notes, invoices and service agreements.

This policy is free. You can read it in the app (Privacy) or at https://help.supportduo.com.au/privacy/. If you'd like it in another format, such as large print, Easy Read, plain text or read aloud, email contactsupportduo@gmail.com. The app can also read any screen aloud.

2. Who SupportDuo is for, and people acting for someone else

SupportDuo accounts are for adults (18 and over). A participant who is under 18, or who wants or needs help to manage their supports, can have a parent, guardian, NDIS plan nominee or other authorised person set up and run the account for them. That person gives the participant’s information on their behalf and agrees to this policy for them.

If you act for someone else, please involve them in decisions as much as they are able, respect their wishes, and only share what is needed. If you need help to make decisions, you can use a supporter, advocate or nominee.

If we learn that someone under 18 has opened an account without a parent or guardian, we will contact them and may close the account. Contact contactsupportduo@gmail.com if you are concerned.

4. Sensitive information and your consent

Some of what SupportDuo holds is sensitive information under the Privacy Act: disability and support needs, allergies, health details a worker records in shift notes or incident reports, gender identity, interests that may show religious beliefs, and qualifications such as worker screening checks.

We only collect sensitive information with your consent (or the consent of the person acting for you) and only because the app needs it: to match participants with suitable workers, to keep people safe, and to run the support relationship. We never use it for advertising or for anything unrelated.

You can change or remove most of it in the app at any time, and stop sharing your NDIS or safety details with a worker whenever you like. If you remove information the app needs, some features may stop working well; for example, matching is less accurate without support needs.

5. Information about other people

SupportDuo sometimes holds information about people who gave it to us through someone else:

  • emergency contacts, plan managers and nominees a participant enters;
  • shift notes and incident reports a support worker writes about a participant (the participant does not see these in the app);
  • people named in a safety report or incident report; and
  • a participant’s details entered by a parent, guardian or nominee.

If you give us someone else’s information, please make sure they know and are happy for you to share it. That person can contact us to see or correct it.

6. How we use your information

  • to create and secure your account and sign you in;
  • to show participants suitable support workers nearby, and workers suitable participants, and to work out shared interests and a match score;
  • to run connection requests, messages, meetings, service agreements and notifications;
  • to give support workers tools for shift notes, NDIS invoices, income, expenses and tax estimates;
  • to check that a support worker’s ABN is active;
  • to keep people safe: safety details, blocking, safety reports and incident records;
  • to manage subscriptions and what each plan includes;
  • to answer your questions, support and privacy requests; and
  • to meet our legal obligations and protect the service from misuse.

9. Automated matching and decisions

SupportDuo uses computer rules, with no person reviewing each result, to decide who appears in your search results and in what order. These decisions affect who you can find and connect with.

These are decided automatically:

  • who is shown: only people on the other side, within each other’s travel distance, who haven’t blocked each other and don’t already have a request open;
  • a participant’s gender comfort choice: in “only show” mode, workers outside that choice are not shown and can’t send that participant a request;
  • whether a support worker is taking new participants;
  • if switched on, whether a support worker’s ABN is active, and whether a member has a current plan or has reached their plan’s limit, which decide whether they are shown and can make new connections; and
  • whether a support worker can use the Invoices & Income tools (Premium plan).

These help people decide, but don’t decide for them: the order of search results and the match score, which is 60% how many of a participant’s support needs a worker offers and 40% shared interests, then distance. Interests never stop anyone being shown.

The personal information used is: your area and travel distance, support needs or supports offered, interests, gender and gender comfort choice, blocks, ABN status and plan status. If you think an automated result is wrong or unfair, email contactsupportduo@gmail.com and a person will look into it.

11. How we keep it safe

  • All information travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and our hosting provider encrypts stored data.
  • Database rules let each person see only the records they are allowed to, as described above.
  • Files such as photos and invoices are private and opened through short-lived links.
  • Sign-in uses emailed one-time links rather than passwords, and the app can be locked with Face ID, fingerprint, passcode or PIN.
  • Only a few authorised people can access the systems, and only when needed.

If a data breach is likely to cause serious harm, we will tell the people affected and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, as the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme requires, and explain what you can do.

12. How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is open, and delete or de-identify information we no longer need. When you delete your account:

  • your profile, photo, answers, location, preferences, safety and NDIS details, subscription records and notification tokens are deleted straight away;
  • your connections are deleted, which also deletes the messages, meetings and service agreements you had with each person, for both of you, so save a PDF of any agreement you want to keep first;
  • a support worker’s own records (shift notes, incident reports, invoices, expenses, receipts and tax settings) are deleted, so workers should export anything they must keep for tax or NDIS purposes first.

Some information is kept after an account is deleted:

  • Safety reports: a report you made is kept without your name; a report about you is kept with your name and email, so reports of harm can’t be erased. We keep these for as long as needed for safety and legal reasons, up to 7 years.
  • A support worker’s business records about a participant (shift notes, incident reports and invoices with the participant’s name, NDIS number and plan manager details) stay with that worker if the participant deletes their account, because the worker may be legally required to keep them.
  • Security and purchase records (account and subscription identifiers, and records of account deletion and subscription changes) are kept for up to 7 years for fraud prevention, tax and legal reasons.
  • Copies in our hosting provider’s backups are removed as those backups expire.
  • Anything already sent outside the app, such as an emailed invoice or a saved PDF, isn’t affected.

Deleting your account doesn’t cancel a Google Play or App Store subscription; cancel it in your phone’s subscription settings first.

13. Delete your account

In the app: open Home, scroll down and tap “Delete my account”, then confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app: email contactsupportduo@gmail.com from the email address you sign in with, with the subject “Delete my SupportDuo account”. We will confirm it’s you, delete the account within 30 days (usually much sooner), and email you when it’s done. More details are at https://help.supportduo.com.au/delete-account/.

You can also delete some information without closing your account: remove your photo or About me, change your answers, clear your NDIS or safety details, stop sharing them with a worker, or remove bank details from Invoices & Income.

14. Your choices

  • Change or remove profile information, answers and preferences in the app at any time.
  • Choose which workers can see your NDIS and safety details, and stop sharing at any time.
  • Type your suburb instead of using your phone’s location.
  • Turn notifications, location, camera and photo access on or off in your phone’s settings; the app still works without them.
  • Block someone so they can no longer see or contact you.

15. Seeing and correcting your information

You can see and update most of your information in the app. You can also ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, by emailing contactsupportduo@gmail.com. There is no fee. We may need to confirm who you are, and we will respond within 30 days. If we can’t do what you ask, we will explain why in writing and tell you how to complain. If we don’t agree to correct something, you can ask us to attach a note with your view.

16. When we may share information without asking

We may use or share personal information without asking first only where the law requires or allows it, including:

  • to lessen or prevent a serious threat to someone’s life, health or safety;
  • to report suspected abuse, neglect, exploitation, violence or sexual misconduct to the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission, police or child protection authorities;
  • to comply with a court order, subpoena or a request from a regulator such as the NDIS Commission; and
  • to help police investigate unlawful activity.

17. No selling, advertising, tracking or AI

  • We do not sell or rent personal information.
  • We show no ads and do not share information with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and use no analytics or advertising tools.
  • No artificial intelligence (AI) service receives your information or messages.
  • We send service messages (such as sign-in links, notifications and important account notices) but no marketing. If we ever start, we will ask first and every message will have a way to opt out.

18. Questions and complaints

If you have a question or a complaint about how we handle personal information, email contactsupportduo@gmail.com. We will acknowledge it within 7 days, look into it, and give you a written answer within 30 days. You can have an advocate or someone you trust help you.

If you aren’t happy with our answer, you can contact:

  • the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC): www.oaic.gov.au, phone 1300 363 992, GPO Box 5288 Sydney NSW 2001;
  • for concerns about the safety or quality of NDIS supports, or a worker’s conduct: the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission, 1800 035 544, www.ndiscommission.gov.au;
  • for health information in Victoria: the Health Complaints Commissioner, www.hcc.vic.gov.au; in New South Wales: the Information and Privacy Commission, www.ipc.nsw.gov.au.

19. Changes to this policy

We review this policy regularly and whenever the app changes how it handles personal information. The date at the top shows when it last changed. If we make an important change, we will tell you in the app before it applies.
